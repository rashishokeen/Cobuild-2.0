OUR STORY

Started by builders,
for builders.

CoBuild was born out of a simple frustration: great ideas die because the right people never find each other. We've all been there — a killer concept, no one to build it with.

So we built the community we wished existed. A space where ideas get validated, co-founders get matched, and projects actually get shipped. No fluff. Just builders.

01 · INAUGURAL EDITION CodeBuild 1.0 · 512K+ Impressions 3,387 student teams nationwide, culminating at IGDTUW, New Delhi.
↗

↗
